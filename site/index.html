Area within reach

Land area with at least one reachable street, counted in 100 m × 100 m squares. For scale, the Municipality of Athens covers about 39 km².

How car travel times are modelled

There is no live traffic feed here, so driving uses a typical-weekday model: free-flow speed by road type, multiplied by a congestion factor that follows a working day (morning peak 08:00–09:00, afternoon peak 14:30–15:30, a smaller evening peak, light traffic late at night) and is stronger on arterials than on motorways or side streets. One-way streets are respected and every junction adds a delay. Inside ~2.5 km of Syntagma travel is a further 15% slower.

With parking on, the trip is: 2 min to reach the car, drive to a parking spot, spend time finding a space there (8 min within 2 km of Syntagma, 5 min within 4 km, 3 min within 8 km, 1 min further out), then walk to the destination. Parking a little further away and walking is allowed, and so is not taking the car at all. On the car map, faint streets are ones the car drives past; solid streets are ones you have actually arrived at.
